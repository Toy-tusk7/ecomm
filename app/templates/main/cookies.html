1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your computer or mobile device when you visit websites. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently and to compile analytical reports.

2. Types of Cookies We Use

We utilize two primary types of cookies:

  • Essential Session Cookies: These cookies are critical to operating our platform. They maintain your shopping cart contents and track user sessions for logged-in accounts. They do not track your activity across other sites and are deleted upon closing the browser.
  • Analytical Cookies (Google Analytics): If you consent via our cookie banner, we load Google Analytics. This places tracking cookies to compile aggregated, anonymous metrics regarding page load performance, scroll depths, and user navigation patterns.
