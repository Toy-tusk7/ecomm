The specs.

Every component chosen for performance, hackability, and longevity.

01
LoRa Spread Spectrum Radio
SX1276 chirp spread spectrum defeats multipath interference and flies clean through urban RF environments that cripple 2.4GHz systems. Swap a BOM line for 915MHz (FCC) or 433MHz (ETSI).
02
ExpressLRS Native Protocol
Betaflight, iNav, Ardupilot — all supported out of the box. Flash firmware over USB-C with the official ELRS Configurator in under two minutes.
03
Raspberry Pi Pico RP2040
264KB SRAM, 2MB flash, 26 GPIO pins. Headroom for OSD data forwarding, telemetry relay, and MAVLink bridging simultaneously.
04
Fully Open Hardware
Every schematic, PCB layout, BOM, and firmware file is open source. Fork it, extend it, manufacture your own. No black boxes.
05
Ultra-Low Power Standby
LoRa sleep current under 1µA. Combined with Pico's dormant mode, total idle draw drops below 100µA.
06
USB-C Flash & Debug
Native USB-C for UF2 firmware flashing and CDC serial debug. Drag-and-drop or CLI. Works on Windows, macOS, Linux.
