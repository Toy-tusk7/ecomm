1. Introduction

Welcome to TEDH ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website, purchase our ELRS Pico LoRa Controller, or interact with our services.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us when using our store, making a purchase, or submitting an inquiry:

  • Personal Identifiers: Name, email address, mailing address, billing address, and account credentials.
  • Inquiry Details: Company name, project briefs, and type of cooperation requested via our contact form.
  • Transaction Information: Details about payments (processed securely via mock gateways) and order history.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for various purposes, including to:

  • Process, fulfill, and manage your orders and cart details.
  • Respond to product collaborations, partnerships, and general inquiries.
  • Monitor site performance and user flows using analytics to improve usability.
  • Comply with legal obligations and prevent fraudulent activities.

4. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use essential session cookies to manage cart states and user logins. Additionally, subject to your consent, we employ analytical cookies (Google Analytics) to compile anonymous reports on page views and system load. For detailed information, please refer to our Cookie Policy.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy or our data practices, please start a conversation on our Contact Page or reach out directly via email at hello@tedh.in.
